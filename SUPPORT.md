# Support

## Free

Open a GitHub issue for bugs and questions. Responses are best effort with no committed turnaround.

Don't attach client drawings, registers or other project documents. Issues are public.

## Paid

Setup, customisation and ongoing support for construction and fit-out firms are available through
Invero Projects.

Setup covers registering your projects, matching the config to your folder structure and drawing
stages, and a first scan walked through with your PM.

Customisation covers your checklists, register format, report layout, cost plan labels and sign-off
gates.

Support covers fixes, updates as Claude Code changes, and help when a scan or stage doesn't behave.

Work is quoted per firm. Email james@inveroprojects.com.au with the number of projects and how your
project folders are set out.
