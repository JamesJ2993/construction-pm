# General Contracting Requirements

**DRAFT — for review.** Standing {{tender}} requirements, common to every package.

Inserted verbatim as **section 4** of the {{scope_of_works}} `.docx` by `scope-of-works-agent`.
Numbered `G1…` — a sequence deliberately separate from the site-specific scope numbering, so that
adding a requirement here can never shift a site-specific item number.

**These are contractual.** They allocate risk and obligation, and a {{tenderer}} prices against
them. **A firm adopts this text once, adapted to its jurisdiction and its standard contract
position, before first issue** — and reviews it again after any change to that position. Where the
head contract says something different, the head contract governs — flag the conflict rather than
issuing both. Where the firm keeps its own general requirements (`templates.dir`), those are used
instead of this file.

**Project-specific values are bracketed** — `[close date]`, `[site address]`, `[hours]`. The agent
fills these from the project documents, or lists them as gaps if the documents do not state them.
It does not invent them. `{{key}}` terms are written in the project's terminology.

---

## G1 — {{Tender}} submission requirements

G1.1 The {{Tenderer}} shall submit a conforming {{tender}} in accordance with these requirements. A
{{tender}} that does not comply may be set aside at the {{client}}'s discretion.

G1.2 {{Tender}}s close at `[close time]` on `[close date]` and shall be lodged to
`[lodgement address]`. Late {{tender}}s may not be considered.

G1.3 The {{Tenderer}} shall submit a fully priced Scope Schedule in the format issued, with every
item priced. An unpriced item will be taken as included in the {{tender}} sum at no additional cost.

G1.4 The {{Tenderer}} shall state, against each item of the Scope Schedule, the drawing it has
priced the item from.

G1.5 The {{Tenderer}} shall state all exclusions, qualifications and assumptions on which its price
relies. An exclusion not stated at {{tender}} is not available after award.

G1.6 The {{Tenderer}} shall acknowledge receipt of each addendum issued during the {{tender}} period,
by number, in its return. A {{tender}} that does not acknowledge every addendum is incomplete.

G1.7 The {{Tenderer}} shall submit: a {{tender}} sum breakdown by trade; a {{preliminaries}}
breakdown; an indicative construction {{schedule}} showing the critical path and key milestones; a
schedule of proposed subcontractors; rates for {{change_plural}}, including labour rates,
percentage additions for overhead and profit, and rates for delay costs; details of insurances
held; and evidence of relevant licences and accreditations.

G1.8 The {{Tenderer}} shall submit any proposed alternative or non-conforming offer separately and in
addition to a conforming {{tender}}. An alternative offered without a conforming {{tender}} may not
be considered.

G1.9 The {{Tenderer}} shall satisfy itself as to the full content of the documents forming part of
this scope. No scope item is limited to any one drawing, and reliance on a partial review of the
design is not a ground for a claim.

G1.10 The {{Tenderer}} shall direct all queries during the {{tender}} period to the {{client}}'s
representative in writing. Where a query reveals an ambiguity, the response will be issued to all
{{tenderer}}s by addendum.

G1.11 {{Tender}}s shall remain open for acceptance for `[validity period]` days from the close date.

G1.12 The {{client}} is not bound to accept the lowest or any {{tender}}, and is not liable for any
cost incurred by a {{tenderer}} in preparing its {{tender}}.

## G2 — Safety

G2.1 The Contractor shall comply with the construction safety law applicable at the site
(`[safety regime — project_facts.safety_regime]`), including its regulations and every code,
standard or rule made under it.

G2.2 The Contractor has management and control of the site and shall discharge every duty the
safety law places on the party in that position, including preparing and maintaining the
{{safety_plan}} for the site.

G2.3 The Contractor shall prepare, and obtain acceptance of, a written method statement or job
hazard analysis for each item of high-risk construction work before that work commences.

G2.4 The Contractor shall ensure every person on site holds the safety training the law requires
for construction work, and shall conduct a site-specific induction for every person before they
commence work.

G2.5 The Contractor shall notify the {{client}} immediately of any incident it must report to the
safety regulator, and shall provide a written report and the outcome of its investigation.

G2.6 The Contractor shall maintain the site register, hazard register, and records of inductions,
toolbox talks and inspections, and shall make them available to the {{client}} on request.

G2.7 The Contractor shall supply, install and maintain all safety measures required for the works,
including edge protection, barricading, signage, exclusion zones, temporary lighting and fire
protection.

G2.8 The Contractor shall hold and maintain the insurances required by the Contract, and shall
provide certificates of insurance before commencing work on site.

## G3 — Site requirements

G3.1 The site is `[site address]`. The Contractor shall inspect the site before submitting its
{{tender}} and shall satisfy itself as to the conditions affecting the works.

G3.2 Working hours are `[hours]`. Work outside these hours requires the prior approval of the
{{client}} and, where required, of the relevant authority.

G3.3 The Contractor shall coordinate all site access, deliveries, loading dock bookings and lift
bookings with the building owner or manager, and shall bear the cost of doing so.

G3.4 The Contractor shall provide its own site accommodation, amenities, storage, temporary power,
temporary water, temporary lighting and telecommunications, unless the scope states otherwise.

G3.5 The Contractor shall protect existing works, adjacent tenancies, base building finishes,
services and public areas from damage arising from the works, and shall make good any damage at its
own cost.

G3.6 The Contractor shall provide, maintain and remove all hoarding, screening, propping, scaffold,
temporary works and access equipment required for the works.

G3.7 The Contractor shall make good after its own work and after other trades where the scope so
provides.

G3.8 The Contractor shall remove all waste from site progressively, shall not accumulate waste on
site, and shall dispose of it lawfully. The Contractor shall keep the site clean throughout the works
and shall carry out a builder's clean and a final clean before handover.

G3.9 The Contractor shall control noise, dust, vibration, odour and light spill so as not to disrupt
adjacent occupants, and shall comply with any building rules applying to the site.

G3.10 The Contractor shall coordinate its work with other contractors engaged directly by the
{{client}}, and shall allow for that coordination in its {{schedule}} and price.

## G4 — Permits and authority requirements

G4.1 The Contractor shall obtain, pay for and comply with all permits, approvals and licences
required to carry out the works, other than those the {{client}} has obtained and identified in the
scope.

G4.2 The Contractor shall give all notices required by any authority and shall arrange and attend
all inspections required during the course of the works.

G4.3 The Contractor shall comply with the conditions of the {{planning_approval}}, the
{{building_permit}} and any other approval applying to the works.

G4.4 The Contractor shall obtain and comply with all permits required by the building owner or
manager, including hot works, confined space, isolation, penetration and after-hours permits.

G4.5 The Contractor shall obtain the approval of the relevant authority or building manager before
any isolation, shutdown or interruption of a live service, and shall give the {{client}}
`[notice period]` written notice.

G4.6 The Contractor shall obtain and provide to the {{client}} all certificates, compliance
certificates and approvals required for occupation and use of the works, including the
{{occupancy}} where the Contractor is responsible for it.

## G5 — Quality, inspection and handover

G5.1 The Contractor shall implement a quality management system for the works and shall provide its
quality plan before commencing work on site.

G5.2 The Contractor shall prepare Inspection and Test Plans for each element of the works,
nominating hold points and witness points, and shall obtain acceptance of them before the relevant
work commences.

G5.3 The Contractor shall give the {{client}} `[notice period]` notice of each hold point and shall
not proceed past a hold point without release.

G5.4 The Contractor shall carry out all testing required by the specification and by any authority,
and shall provide test results and certificates.

G5.5 The Contractor shall provide samples, prototypes and mock-ups where the specification requires
them, and shall obtain approval before proceeding with the relevant work.

G5.6 Materials and workmanship shall comply with the specification, the building code adopted for
the site and the standards it references. Where neither states a requirement, materials shall be new
and fit for purpose and workmanship shall be to the standard of a competent contractor in the
relevant trade.

G5.7 The Contractor shall commission all systems and shall provide commissioning records and
certificates.

G5.8 The Contractor shall provide training to the {{client}}'s nominated personnel in the operation
and maintenance of the installed systems.

G5.9 The Contractor shall provide, before {{completion}}: {{om_manuals}}; as-built drawings;
warranties and guarantees; certificates and approvals; commissioning records; and the spares and
attic stock required by the specification.

G5.10 The Contractor shall rectify all defects notified during the {{defects_period}} at its own
cost, and shall carry out that work at times that suit the occupied use of the premises.
