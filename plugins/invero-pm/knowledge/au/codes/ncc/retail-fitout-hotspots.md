# NCC 2025 — where it bites on a retail fitout

A Class 6 tenancy fitout inside an existing building touches a small, predictable set of clauses.
This is the list to run a drawing set against, in the order the issues usually surface.

## Section D — access and egress

| Clause | What to check |
|---|---|
| D1P10, D3D31 | **New in 2025: wayfinding signage.** Check the set actually shows it. A new requirement is the one most likely to be missing from a design carried over from an earlier job. |
| D1P2 | Ramp handrail applicability — a limitation was introduced in 2025 |
| D3D11, D3D22, D3D27 | Threshold ramps, step ramps, stairway identification — all amended in 2025 |
| D2D8 | Fire compartments served by a horizontal exit |

Egress is the usual fitout fight: travel distance to an exit, whether the shopfront line changes the
path of travel, and whether joinery or a queuing area narrows a required width. Measure it on the
plan; do not accept "complies" without a dimension.

## Section E — services and equipment

| Clause | What to check |
|---|---|
| E1D2–E1D9 | Sprinkler coverage. Any ceiling change, bulkhead or high joinery unit is a sprinkler relocation. |
| E2D3, E2D11–D20 | Smoke dampers and ventilation. 2025 aligned terminology with AS 1668.1. |
| E3D4, E3D5, E3D7, E3D11–D12 | Lifts — contrast requirements, fire service key storage |
| E4D8, Specification 25 | **Exit and directional signage.** 2025 updated the requirements; hybrid photoluminescent signs now reference SA TS 5367. |

## Section F — health and amenity

| Clause | What to check |
|---|---|
| F4D4, Table F4D4i | **All-gender facilities are now permitted as an alternative** — an optional pathway, not a mandate. Worth raising with the client early; it changes the amenities layout. |
| F4D6, F4D8 | **Sanitary product disposal and dispensers now required.** Easy to miss on a set drawn to an older standard. |
| F1D2–F1D15 | Water management — Part F1 was restructured in 2025 and **Part F3 was deleted into it**. A citation to F3 is now stale. |
| F6D6, F6D12 | Kitchen exhaust hoods — broadened in 2025. Relevant wherever a tenancy has a food offer. |
| F8D3, F8D5–D6 | Condensation management |

## Section J — energy efficiency

The largest set of 2025 changes, and the one most likely to invalidate a design reused from a
pre-2025 store.

| Clause | What to check |
|---|---|
| **J7D3–D4** | **Lighting control: motion detectors replaced with demand-operated controls.** A retail lighting design carried over from a 2022-era store will not comply as drawn. |
| **J9D5** | **On-site solar photovoltaic systems mandated.** Usually a base building matter, but confirm who carries it — it is a real cost and a real coordination item. |
| J9D3 | Energy monitoring system required |
| J6D3–D13 | Air-conditioning and mechanical ventilation efficiency; pump and chiller requirements added |
| J5D2–D8 | **Climate zone concessions removed.** A concession relied on in an earlier job may no longer exist. |
| J3D5–D14, J4D3–D6 | Thermal performance, solar admittance, insulation U- and R-values |
| J1V1–V4 | Verification methods — the "Design & As Built" modelling option was **removed** in 2025 |

**J7 is the sheet that usually exists in the set** (often drawn as a lighting power density
calculation). Treat an error inside a J7 calculation as higher priority than an equivalent error in
an ordinary schedule: a quantity mistake inside a compliance calculation with a thin margin can flip
the result, and it will not be caught downstream.

## Section C — fire resistance

| Clause | What to check |
|---|---|
| C4D15(2)(b) | Opening protection methods, referencing AS 1668.1 |
| C4D10(d) | Concession extended to wiring and sensors for stair pressurisation |
| C1V3, S7C4 | Fire hazard property and EW classification testing must now be by an **Accredited Testing Laboratory** — check test certificates for linings and finishes name one |
| C3D13(2)(b) | Pressurising equipment for exit parts |

Fire hazard properties of linings, floor coverings and joinery finishes are the fitout's usual
Section C exposure. Ask for the certificate, check the laboratory, check the date.

## How to use this on a review

0. **Confirm the jurisdiction, and check what it varies** — see
   [state-variations.md](state-variations.md). Every clause below is the *national* provision. In
   NSW all nine Parts of Section J are varied, so J7D3-D4 and J9D5 below are not the governing text
   there. SA varies most of fire, egress and amenity. Reading a national clause in a state that
   varied it is the fastest way to be confidently wrong.
1. Confirm the governing edition — see [adoption-and-editions.md](adoption-and-editions.md). If
   the job is pre-2025, most of the "new in 2025" items above do not apply.
2. Work Section D, then E, then F, then J. That order roughly matches how issues cascade: egress
   drives layout, services follow layout, amenities follow services, energy follows all of it.
3. For each finding, cite the clause. A register entry that says "does not comply with the NCC" is
   not actionable; one that says "J7D3 requires demand-operated control, set shows motion detection"
   is.
4. Anything relying on a Performance Solution goes on the register regardless of whether it looks
   sound — it needs a report, an assessment method, and a certifier's acceptance.
