# NCC 2025 state and territory variations

**A clause that reads one way nationally may be amended or deleted in the state the job sits in.**
Citing the unvaried clause is a confidently wrong answer, which is why this file exists.

Variations live in the Volume One schedules:

| Schedule | Jurisdiction |
|---|---|
| 3 | Commonwealth |
| 4 | ACT |
| **5** | **New South Wales** |
| 6 | NT |
| **7** | **Queensland** |
| **8** | **South Australia** |
| 9 | Tasmania |
| **10** | **Victoria** |
| **11** | **Western Australia** |

URL pattern: `/editions/ncc-2025/adopted/volume-one/<schedule-number>-<state-name>`

## What this file records, and what it does not

It records **which Parts each state varies** — verified against the ABCB schedule indexes on
21.08.2026. That is the triage: it tells you where to look.

It does **not** record what each variation says. That needs reading the individual clause pages, and
is recorded as an open gap (`gap-ncc-variation-detail`). Do not infer the content of a variation from
the fact that one exists.

## NSW — Schedule 5

**The most heavily varied jurisdiction, by a distance.** 38 entries.

| Group | Parts varied |
|---|---|
| Specifications | Spec 7 Fire hazard properties · Spec 20 Smoke detection and alarm systems · Spec 43 Bushfire (Class 9) |
| Governing | A2 Compliance with the NCC · A5 Documentation of design and construction · A6 Building classification |
| Structure | B1 |
| **Fire resistance** | **C1 · C2 · C3 · C4** — all four Parts |
| **Access and egress** | **D2 Provision for escape · D3 Construction of exits · D4 Access for people with a disability** |
| **Services** | **E2 Smoke hazard management · E4 Visibility in an emergency, exit signs and warning systems** |
| **Health and amenity** | **F4 Sanitary and other facilities · F6 Light and ventilation · F8 Condensation management** |
| Ancillary | G1 · G5 · G7 |
| Special use | I1 Class 9b · I4 Entertainment venues · I5 Temporary structures · I6 Drive-in theatres |
| **Energy efficiency** | **J1 · J2 · J3 · J4 · J5 · J6 · J7 · J8 · J9 — every Part of Section J** |

### The finding that matters most

**NSW varies all nine Parts of Section J.** NCC 2025's largest fitout changes are in Section J —
J7D3–D4 lighting control moving to demand-operated controls, J9D5 mandating on-site solar, J9D3
energy monitoring, and the removal of climate zone concessions in J5.

So on a NSW job, **every one of those national provisions must be read against the NSW variation
before it is cited.** The national clause is not the governing text. This is the single highest-value
line in this file, because NSW is where most of the current work sits.

NSW also varies A5 (documentation of design and construction), which interacts with the Design and
Building Practitioners regime — see `permitting/nsw/approval-pathways.md`.

| Ancillary | G1 Minor structures and components · G5 Construction in bushfire prone areas |
| **Energy efficiency** | **J6 Air-conditioning and ventilation · J8 Heated water supply and swimming pool and spa pool plant** |

A light touch. For fitout the live ones are **J6** — relevant wherever the tenancy has its own

## SA — Schedule 8

Heavily varied, second only to NSW.

| Group | Parts varied |
|---|---|
| Governing | A6 Building classification |
| Structure | B1 |
| **Fire resistance** | **C2 · C3 Compartmentation and separation · C4 Protection of openings** |
| **Access and egress** | **D2 · D3 · D4 Access for people with a disability** |
| **Services** | **E1 Fire fighting equipment · E2 Smoke hazard management · E4 Exit signs and warning systems** |
| **Health and amenity** | **F1 Water management · F2 Wet areas and overflow protection · F4 Sanitary and other facilities · F5 Room heights · F6 Light and ventilation** |
| Ancillary | G1 · G5 · G7 · G8 Access for maintenance · G9 |
| Special use | I3 Farm buildings · I4 Bulk grain storage |

Fitout exposure is broad: fire resistance, all of egress, fire services, and most of health and
amenity. Note **F1 and F2** — NCC 2025 restructured Part F1 and deleted Part F3 into it, and SA
varies F1, so water management on an SA job needs both the 2025 restructure and the SA variation
read together.

**Section J is not varied in South Australia.**

## How to use this

1. **Get the jurisdiction from the registry** before citing anything. It is recorded per project for
   this reason.
2. **Check this table** for whether the Part you are about to cite is varied in that state.
3. If it is varied, **read the state schedule clause**, not the national one, and cite accordingly.
4. If it is not varied, the national provision governs as written.

Fastest summary, by fitout exposure:

- **NSW** — assume almost everything is varied. Especially all of Section J.
- **SA** — assume fire, egress, services and amenity are varied. Section J is not.
- **VIC** — check egress, E1 and amenity. Section J is not varied.
- **QLD** — check J6 and J8 only.
- **WA** — check F9 only.
