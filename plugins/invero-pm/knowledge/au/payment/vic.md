# Security of Payment — Victoria

The governing Act is the **Building and Construction Industry Security of Payment Act 2002 (Vic)**.
This entry exists so that a Victorian question is never answered from the NSW or Queensland file by
default. It orients; the Act decides.

**Provisional.** This summary was written from a practice procedure that was itself written from the
amended Act, not read against the Act for this pack. What would verify it: a person opens the
current consolidated Act on legislation.vic.gov.au, confirms each point below, and the curator moves
this file to `verified` with the date the Act was read.

## The regime changed on 15 April 2026

The **Building Legislation Amendment (Fairer Payments on Jobsites and Other Matters) Act 2025 (Vic)**
commenced on 15 April 2026 and substantially amended the Act, **with retrospective effect on existing
contracts**. Anyone administering a Victorian contract on pre-April 2026 habits will get it wrong.

What changed, in outline:

- **Excluded amounts and reference dates are abolished.** Claims that could not previously be made
  under the Act can now be made. Variations and time-related costs are claimable and are assessed on
  their merits against the contract.
- **Payment terms are capped**, and the cap runs from service, not from when someone opened the email.
- **A reason omitted from a payment schedule cannot be raised later in an adjudication.** Every reason
  — valuation, defects, incomplete work, set-off, liquidated damages, back charges — has to be in the
  schedule.
- **Notice-based time bars can be declared unfair under s 13A.** A failed notice is therefore a
  finding, not an ending: assess the merits as well, and record that you did. A recommendation to
  reject on notice alone, with nothing behind it, is the one most likely to be overturned.

At August 2026 two minor provisions of the amending Act had not commenced and could be further
amended. Confirm the current position with the client's lawyers on any significant claim. That
caution travels with this file.

## Why no dates here

Deliberately. Getting a date wrong by one day is worse than not having it, and a date copied into a
knowledge file drifts out of step with the Act. The payment schedule deadline and the payment due
date are read from the Act at project setup, recorded in `project_facts`, and the contract can set a
shorter period than the statutory maximum, so the contract governs alongside the Act.

**Business days.** Victoria's count carries its own end-of-year exclusion, which is wider than
NSW's. Count on the Act's definition, never on another jurisdiction's habit.

## Where the project manager sits

The project manager assesses and drafts; **the Principal serves**. The PM confirms in writing that
the schedule has been served and records the date and method. Nothing in this file changes that, and
no agent serves anything.

## Interstate work

The Victorian position **does not transfer**. On any interstate project, establish the applicable
Act and its timeframes at project setup and record them in the contract calendar and the project's
`project_facts` — see the jurisdiction file in this folder.
